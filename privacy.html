Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TRENDR CreatorFlow handles information when connecting to supported social media platforms.

Information processed

CreatorFlow may process platform account identifiers, authorization tokens, publishing status information, and media selected for publishing.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, transfer owner-approved media, check publishing status, prevent duplicate uploads, and maintain the CreatorFlow publishing workflow.

Storage

CreatorFlow is primarily a desktop application. Authentication credentials and workflow state are stored locally on the authorized user's computer.

Sharing

CreatorFlow does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to a social platform only when required to provide an explicitly authorized platform integration.

TikTok integration

When authorized, CreatorFlow may use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to authenticate a TikTok account and transfer approved media to TikTok as a draft for final review and posting by the account owner.

Revoking access

Platform access can be revoked through the relevant platform account settings. Stored authorization credentials may also be removed locally.
