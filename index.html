CreatorFlow

TRENDR CreatorFlow is a desktop workflow used to prepare, review and distribute approved short-form video content across supported social platforms.

What CreatorFlow does

CreatorFlow helps organise source media, prepare vertical video edits, generate captions and publishing metadata, perform technical quality checks, and send owner-approved media to supported platform publishing workflows.

How publishing works

Content is reviewed before distribution. Platform integrations use official APIs and authorization flows. For TikTok, CreatorFlow is designed to send an approved local video to TikTok as a draft using the Content Posting API. Final review and posting remain inside TikTok.

Privacy and security

CreatorFlow is designed to request only the platform permissions required for its publishing workflow. Authentication credentials are stored locally on the authorized user's computer and are not sold to third parties.
